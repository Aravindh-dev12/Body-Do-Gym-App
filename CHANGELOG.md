## [Unreleased]

### Added

- **Body Do Brand & Dumbbell Logo.** Full rebranding to Body Do with custom vertical dumbbell iconography across the app, splash screen, favicon, and PWA icons.
- **Black & Athletic Red Theme.** Pitch-black background (`#000000`), dark charcoal surfaces, and vibrant athletic red accent highlights (`#ff2a40`).
- **Collapsible Sidebar.** Collapsible desktop navigation rail supporting both expanded full-width view and compact icon rail with persistence.
- **Account & Profile Modal.** Profile dialog supporting guest status, account creation, and login directly from the sidebar.
- **Expanded Dashboard Layout.** Widened content view (`--content-width: 82rem`) for desktop fitness dashboard viewing.
